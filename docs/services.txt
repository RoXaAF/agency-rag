Bright Studio - Services

Bright Studio is a small creative marketing agency based in Austin, Texas, founded in 2018. We have six team members and work with local businesses and startups. We offer four core services.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Our SEO service helps your website rank higher on Google. It includes a technical site audit, keyword research for up to 30 target keywords, on-page optimization of up to 15 pages, monthly backlink outreach (5 new quality links per month), and a monthly ranking report. SEO results usually take 3 to 6 months to become visible. Our SEO lead is Priya Nair.

Branding
Our branding service builds a complete visual identity. It includes a logo design with three initial concepts and two rounds of revisions, a color palette, typography selection, a brand voice guide, and a 20-page brand guidelines PDF. A standard branding project takes 4 to 6 weeks. Branding is a one-off project, though it is also included in the Growth and Premier packages. Our brand strategist is Marcus Webb.

Social Media Management
Our social media service covers Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. It includes a monthly content calendar, 12 designed posts per month, community management (replying to comments and messages within one business day), and a monthly analytics summary. We do not manage TikTok or X (Twitter) accounts. Our social media manager is Elena Rossi.

Web Design
Our web design service creates responsive websites of up to 10 pages, built on WordPress or Webflow. It includes wireframes, two design revisions, mobile optimization, basic on-page SEO setup, and 30 days of free support after launch. A typical website takes 6 to 8 weeks to complete. E-commerce stores are not included in the standard service and are quoted separately. Our lead web designer is Daniel Okafor.

What we do not offer
Bright Studio does not offer paid advertising management (such as Google Ads or Meta Ads), video production, public relations, or legal and accounting services. If a client needs these, we can recommend trusted partner companies.
