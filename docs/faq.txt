Bright Studio - Frequently Asked Questions

How does onboarding work?
Onboarding has four steps. Step 1: a free 30-minute discovery call with Aisha Rahman. Step 2: we send a proposal within 3 business days. Step 3: you sign the contract and pay the first invoice. Step 4: a kickoff meeting within 5 business days of signing, where we collect brand assets and logins.

How long until I see results?
Social media engagement usually improves within 4 to 8 weeks. SEO takes 3 to 6 months. A new website takes 6 to 8 weeks to build. A branding project takes 4 to 6 weeks.

How long are contracts?
Contract length depends on the package: Starter is 3 months, Growth is 6 months, and Premier is 12 months. The standalone SEO service is month-to-month. One-time projects (branding, web design, Growth Audit) have no ongoing contract.

How do I cancel?
After the minimum term, you can cancel at any time with 30 days written notice by emailing aisha@brightstudio.example. Cancelling before the minimum term ends requires payment of the remaining months at 50% of the monthly fee. Month-to-month services can be cancelled with 14 days notice.

Do I own the work you create?
Yes. Once the final invoice is paid, you own all logos, designs, website files and written content. We keep the right to show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Can I pause my package?
You can pause a package once per year for up to 2 months, if you have completed the minimum term. A paused package costs nothing, but your social media posting and SEO work stop during the pause.

Do you work with clients outside Texas?
Yes. Most of our work is done remotely. Meetings are held over video call, and in-person meetings are available for clients in the Austin area.

Do you run paid ads?
No. Bright Studio does not manage paid advertising. We can refer you to partner agencies.

What are your office hours?
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM Central Time. Emails are answered within one business day.

What if I am not happy with the work?
Tell your account manager, Aisha Rahman. We will fix issues within the agreed revision rounds at no cost. If you are still not satisfied after the first 30 days of a monthly package, we will refund that month's fee.
