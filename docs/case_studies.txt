Bright Studio - Case Studies

Here are three recent client projects and their measured results.

Case Study 1: Maple & Oak Bakery (local bakery, Austin)
Goal: Increase online orders and grow local social media following.
Package: Growth Package, 8 months.
Work done: Instagram and Facebook content, local SEO, a brand refresh with new packaging labels.
Results: Online orders increased by 62% in 8 months. Instagram followers grew from 1,200 to 5,400. The bakery's Google Maps ranking for "bakery Austin" moved from position 14 to position 3.

Case Study 2: PeakFit Gym (fitness studio, Round Rock)
Goal: Fill more membership slots before a second location opened.
Package: Premier Package, 12 months.
Work done: Full rebrand, a new 8-page website, full SEO, and social media on all three platforms.
Results: Membership sign-ups rose by 41% in the first 6 months. Website traffic grew by 180%. The new website loads in 1.8 seconds, down from 6.2 seconds on the old site. The gym reached full capacity at the second location in 4 months.

Case Study 3: LedgerLeaf (accounting software startup)
Goal: Generate more qualified sign-ups for a free trial.
Package: Standalone SEO service, 10 months, month-to-month.
Work done: Keyword research, technical audit, 20 blog articles optimized for search, and backlink outreach.
Results: Organic traffic grew by 240%. Free-trial sign-ups from search increased from 35 per month to 118 per month. Three target keywords reached the first page of Google.
