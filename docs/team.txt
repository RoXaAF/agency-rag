Bright Studio - Our Team

Bright Studio has six team members. Here is who does what.

Sarah Chen - Founder and Creative Director
Sarah founded Bright Studio in 2018 after eight years at a large advertising firm in Chicago. She leads overall strategy, reviews all major creative work, and hosts the monthly strategy calls for Premier clients. Specialty: brand strategy and client relationships. Contact: sarah@brightstudio.example

Marcus Webb - Brand Strategist
Marcus leads all branding projects, including logos, brand guidelines and brand voice. He has 10 years of experience in identity design. If you want to talk about branding, Marcus is the person to contact. Contact: marcus@brightstudio.example

Priya Nair - SEO Lead
Priya manages keyword research, technical audits, and the monthly SEO ranking reports. She previously worked in-house at an e-commerce company. Specialty: technical SEO and local search. Contact: priya@brightstudio.example

Elena Rossi - Social Media Manager
Elena plans and publishes content for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, and handles community management. She has a background in journalism. Specialty: content calendars and short-form video captions. Contact: elena@brightstudio.example

Daniel Okafor - Lead Web Designer
Daniel designs and builds client websites in WordPress and Webflow. Specialty: responsive design and website speed optimization. Contact: daniel@brightstudio.example

Aisha Rahman - Account Manager
Aisha is the first point of contact for new clients. She handles onboarding, contracts, invoices, and day-to-day questions about your project. If you want to sign up, ask about billing, or are unsure who to talk to, contact Aisha. Contact: aisha@brightstudio.example
