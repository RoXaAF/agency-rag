Bright Studio - Pricing and Packages

We offer three monthly packages. All prices are in US dollars and are billed monthly. Prices do not include sales tax.

Starter Package - $750 per month
The Starter package is designed for very small businesses. It includes social media management on one platform (12 posts per month) and a monthly analytics summary. It does not include SEO or web design. Minimum contract: 3 months.

Growth Package - $1,800 per month
The Growth package is our most popular option. It includes social media management on all three platforms (Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn), basic SEO (keyword research for up to 15 keywords and on-page optimization of up to 8 pages), and a one-time brand refresh (updated logo files and color palette). Minimum contract: 6 months.

Premier Package - $3,500 per month
The Premier package is our full-service option. It includes everything in Growth, plus full SEO (up to 30 keywords, 15 pages, and 5 backlinks per month), a complete branding project, one new website of up to 10 pages in the first three months, and a dedicated monthly strategy call with the founder. Minimum contract: 12 months.

Note on similar names: the "Growth Package" is a monthly marketing package. The "Growth Audit" is a separate one-time service and is not a package (see below).

One-time and add-on services
Growth Audit: $950, one-time. A written review of your current marketing, delivered within 10 business days. Not a subscription.
Standalone SEO service: $1,200 per month, no long contract required (month-to-month).
Standalone social media management: $950 per month for all three platforms.
Standalone branding project: $4,200, one-time fee.
Standalone web design project: $5,500 for up to 10 pages, one-time fee, paid 50% upfront and 50% on launch.
Extra social media posts: $40 per post beyond the monthly allowance.

Discounts
Nonprofit organizations receive 15% off any monthly package. Paying a package annually in advance gives a 10% discount. Discounts cannot be combined.

Payment
We accept bank transfer (ACH) and credit card. Credit card payments carry a 3% processing fee. Invoices are sent on the 1st of each month and are due within 14 days.
